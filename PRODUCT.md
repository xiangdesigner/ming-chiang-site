## Product Purpose

Ming Chiang Land Economics & Land Management is a multidisciplinary professional group in Taichung, Taiwan, covering land economics, land management, real estate, urban planning, property appraisal, land registration, brokerage and asset valuation. The website exists to make the full scope of the group intelligible in seconds, establish professional trust, and lead a visitor to make contact. Success: a visitor understands what the group does, believes it is qualified, and calls, emails or sends an enquiry.

## Product Principles

1. Scope made legible: the breadth of services is the offer, so structure must let a visitor find their own case in seconds.
2. Proof by specificity, not by claims: licences, entity names, commissioner categories and the academy are the credibility; no invented numbers.
3. Public-sector trustworthy and privately reassuring at once: serious, precise, calm, never salesy.
4. Contact is the conversion: phone, email and address are always one action away.
5. Motion communicates land, measurement and structure; it never decorates for its own sake.
